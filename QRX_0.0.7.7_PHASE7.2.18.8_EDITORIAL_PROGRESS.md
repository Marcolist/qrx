# QRX 0.0.7.7 Phase 7.2.18.8 — Editorial Progress

This phase continues the fail-closed locale-by-locale editorial program.

## Western Europe — Pass 4
French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch received another dedicated long-form pass. The remaining English-identical staking/delegation, BTC Light, VELOCITY/SPV, Kraken, privacy, backup/recovery and QRX-native market explanations identified in the audit were translated where applicable. Product/protocol identifiers remain intentionally stable.

## Central Europe — Pass 3
Polish, Czech and Slovak received an expanded terminology pass for core wallet navigation, send/receive, balances, security, backup/recovery, staking/delegation, validators, rewards, markets and status concepts.

## Safety
No consensus, Genesis, signing, staking accounting, RPC security or wallet-key derivation changes are part of this localization-only phase. Key parity alone never marks a locale as editorially complete.
